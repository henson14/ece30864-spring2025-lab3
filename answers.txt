Question 2:
The SCRUM master leads the product development team and hosts the weekly/daily SCRUM sessions. They are focused on streamlining the development process and monitoring team contributions. The product owner oversees the project and its backlog. They gather customer requirements and work to prioritize what needs work. They are more focused on the lifecycle and future of the software rather than the day-to-day tasks.

Question 3:
Sprint Planning Meeting - determines what needs to be accomplished in the sprint and assigns team members to each task
Standup Meeting - a short check-in meeting where members discuss their progress and any blockers
Sprint Retrospective Meeting - the team reflects on what went well and poorly during the sprint, they determine what should happen with any remaining work from the sprint

Question 4:
Epic - a large component that is made up of several stories
Story - a software system requirement, usually fairly short
Bug - an identified problem with the software that needs to be addressed
Task - a concrete task that needs to be completed, usually lives within epics
